No, traditional Project Managers and Delivery Managers don't belong to the Scrum team. You must hold this line in any organisation and set clear boundaries.

Here's why:

Scrum intentionally distributes traditional PM responsibilities across three roles:

+ Product Owner handles prioritisation and stakeholder management (the "what")
+ Scrum Master facilitates process and removes impediments (the "how we work")
+ Development Team self-organises around execution (the "how we build")
